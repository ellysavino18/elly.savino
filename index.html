About

I'm a marine biologist with a strong background in trophic ecology, spatial analysis and conservation methodologies. I am experienced in multiple stages of the scientific process, from oceanographic fieldwork and laboratory sample processing to ecological data analysis in R. My primary research interests lie at the intersection of ecosystem dynamics, species physiology and behaviour, specifically focusing on how marine organisms adapt to environmental and anthropogenic stressors. I am now seeking to apply my skills and expand my knowledge in a research environment, with the aim of producing data-driven science that can be translated into effective conservation strategies and sustainable marine management.
